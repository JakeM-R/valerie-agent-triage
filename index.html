Use your own export

Load a CSV of agent tasks. It is read in your browser and replaces the demo data on this page; nothing is uploaded or stored. The fix log starts empty for your data.

id, week, date, practice, workflow, channel, outcome, cause, staff_minutes, agent_note
  • outcome is automated, review or staff; cause is empty when automated.
  • week is a whole number; the latest week is “this week”. practice is optional.
  • Causes the page does not know start as Unassigned with 1 day of effort. Set them in the queue.

Demo data. The six practices are fictional. Every task, minute, agent note and fix on this page is generated from a fixed seed. Effort, owners and catch rates are triage estimates, not measurements. Nothing here describes Valerie Health’s customers, agents or performance.
