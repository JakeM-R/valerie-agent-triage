      Use your own export

      Load a CSV with the columns below. It is read in your browser and replaces the demo data on this page. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

      id, week, date, workflow, channel, outcome, cause, staff_minutes, agent_note
      • outcome: automated, review or staff
      • cause: empty when automated; any text otherwise
      • week: whole number (1, 2, 3…); the latest week is treated as “this week”
      • staff_minutes: number, 0 when automated
